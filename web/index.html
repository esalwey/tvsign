    Restore a backup

    Replaces apps, Apple TVs and Apple accounts with those of the backup. Your current ones are kept aside on disk.

    A backup contains secrets: anyone holding it can use your Apple ID session. Keep it private, and never run two tvsign instances with the same Apple ID.
